Tap Start
Scale: hold a credit card flat on the wall, tap Set scale, tap both ends. Then keep the phone at that same distance (~1 ft) and sweep along the wall. Move closer or farther and the size estimate is wrong, so re-set scale. Best results: cabin dark, bright light outside (or the reverse), phone steady.
